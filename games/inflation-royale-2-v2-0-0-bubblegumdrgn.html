Inflation Royale 2 [v2.0.0] [BubblegumDrgn]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-26 · Ongoing · Completed

Inflation Royale is a tactical battle-royale experience where every fight ends in someone getting bigger. Choose three allies, gear up with elemental weapons, and survive a shrinking map while rival squads try to turn you into the next parade float. Combat is turn-based and mean. Weapons carry status effects — sticky, buoyant, juiced, bubbled, and more — that change how the fight plays out. Heavy weapons and shotguns hit entire squads. Tanks can body-block blasts. Snipers can pick people off from across the map.Between fights you move through regions, scavenge loot, manage support items, and decide whether to push, rotate, or set up an ambush before the missiles start falling.
