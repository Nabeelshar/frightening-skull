The Hollow Hunt [v1.0] [TheHollowHunt]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-10-29 · Ongoing · RPGM

Vellmoor is a poor village on the edge of the Black Forest, ruled by the cold and harsh Baron Ostric. Young women are disappearing, and a killer with a lantern walks the woods at night. Teodric is a former clerk, a fearful man haunted by his past. His wife Liva is the opposite: a forester’s daughter, brave, sharp with a sword, and the most beautiful woman in the village. Every man in Vellmoor has his eyes on her, and many don’t hide it. When the Baron sends them to hunt the killer, he gives them a third companion: Lucan, the best sword in the village and its worst character. In front of Teodric he plays the loyal friend. The moment Teodric looks away, the mask comes off. Dark medieval mystery and horror, with a card-based battle system.
