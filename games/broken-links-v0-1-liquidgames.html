Broken Links [v0.1] [LiquidGames]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-15 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

You play Lorcan, a guy who runs the lost property desk at the ferry terminal of a small harbour town. He can see threads between people. Gold means love, red means somebody wants somebody, green is jealousy, grey is a debt. If he touches a thread he gets to feel what they feel for a moment. If he cuts it, they stop feeling it. They still remember everything, they just don’t care anymore. It starts one night at the Halyard Bar, where he spots a thread brighter than anything he’s seen before. It plays as a comic, one panel at a time, with choices in between.
