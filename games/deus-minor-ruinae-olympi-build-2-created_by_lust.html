No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-11 · Ongoing · Unreal Engine

Deus Minor: Ruinæ Olympi is a top-down action RPG inspired in part by Titan Quest, reimagined through its own combat systems, narrative and visual identity. You play as Demetra, raised far from civilization in an effort to restrain her might, unaware of the divine power concealed within her. Upon reaching the mainland, she sets out on a journey across the storied lands of Greek Antiquity, where Olympian gods and monsters hold dominion – a journey that will ultimately lead Demetra to discover who she truly is. Will you bring the gods to ruin, or choose to stand among them? The choice is yours.
