Disaster [v0.7] [Katty]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-16 · Ongoing · HTML

DISASTER is a gritty adult RPG set in the neon-drenched streets of modern Prague, following a young man who wakes up from a seven-month coma to find his life in absolute shambles. Having lost a successful sports career and all his savings due to a tragic accident he was held responsible for, he now faces a crushing $75,000 debt and relentless legal pressure. The game forces you to balance on the edge of survival in an unforgiving simulation where every choice impacts your finances, energy, and sanity as you choose between grueling manual labor and “shady” opportunities within the city’s underworld. Central to the narrative are manipulative therapy sessions with a provocative specialist whose unconventional methods aim to rebuild your shattered confidence, while a fully functional in-game smartphone allows you to navigate social media and adult platforms to forge new connections. This first chapter features a deeply branching story and explicit encounters that, based on your decisions, culminate in one of five unique life paths, determining whether you find a way to redemption or spiral into a definitive downfall.
