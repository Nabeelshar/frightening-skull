A world that looks like ours – but isn’t quite the same. The people who live there carry a different soul for the day and another for the night, and the two trade places each and every day. In a place like this, a game is held – a game that decides the future. Win, and one soul – day or night – gets to monopolize roughly half of her life, never having to trade places again!
