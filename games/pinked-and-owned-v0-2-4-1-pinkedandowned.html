Pinked and Owned [v0.5] [PinkedAndOwned]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-10-16 · Ongoing · HTML

Pinked and Owned is an adult HTML/browser narrative game about slow feminization and a life quietly rearranging itself. You play a guy with a small chastity cage, a complicated roommate, and the kind of job that keeps asking him to wear something new. Stat-driven sandbox, explore the city, build (or lose) your resolve, and watch the story branch based on choices you didn’t even realise were choices. Active solo dev, new content every 2-3 weeks.
