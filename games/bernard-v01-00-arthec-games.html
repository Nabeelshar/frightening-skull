“Pill Fiction (Stories): BERNARD”, a NSFW game about an insecure guy who just started a penis enlargement treatment when two childhood friends unexpectedly move into his apartment. Bernard is a high-quality, handcrafted adventure that pushes the boundaries of what a Ren’Py visual novel can offer, bringing it closer to a classic point-and-click adventure than to a linear “choose your own destiny” novel. Set in the summer of 1999 , the story lets us experience post-Olympic Barcelona through the eyes of Bernard , a poor 23-year-old schmuck ( although he looks more like a hard-lived 40-year-old ), born in the United States. After a childhood and youth plagued by social and romantic failures, he decided to do what any good protagonist would do: run away .
