36 Week [v1.2] [CheckOne]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-09 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

This game tells the story of a young man in his twenties living with his mother, brother, and sister. One day, he receives a message from an unknown number proposing a deal. The next day, a package containing a contract arrives; he foolishly signs it, binding himself to a succubus. Under the terms of this special agreement, he has 36 weeks to receive oral sex from someone he has known for at least a week. If he breaks the contract, he will die.
