Her Test Dates [v0.1.1] [OB Games]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-10 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

Your wife signed up to test a dating app. You built the app. You’re Leo, the backend guy at Glint, a dating app with nine employees and three months of money. Your boss wants real testers for a same-night dating feature, $150 a date. Rent is due Thursday, Maya’s client hasn’t paid in 52 days, and she reads the tester agreement out loud at breakfast and signs it. From then on you get two versions of every date. The one in your console, with check-in and check-out times down to the minute. And the one she tells you on the couch. Sometimes they match. The game is mostly about what you do when they don’t, and what you said to her on Day 6 on the pier. Three routes. Faithful, NTS (you say yes first, she tells you after) and NTR (you find out from timestamps). Choice hints are on for everyone, you can turn them off in settings. All characters depicted are 21 or older.
