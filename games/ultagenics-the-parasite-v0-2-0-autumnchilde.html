Ultagenics – The Parasite [v0.2.0] [AutumnChilde]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-21 · Ongoing · HTML

The world of Ultagenics is a world where a powerful megacorporation controls every aspect of life, and where the people are so used to being controlled that they don’t even realize it anymore. The main character of this story is a Therapist, who uses her intuition and skills to unlock their secret desires, bringing them to the surface, and helping them to embrace their inner desires. The means by which the main character does this is through the use of an alien parasite that has been genetically engineered to be the perfect tool for unlocking and amplifying the desires of its host, and then those around her.
