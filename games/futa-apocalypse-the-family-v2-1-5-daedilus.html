FUTA APOCALYPSE: The Family [v2.1.5] [Daedilus]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-16 · Ongoing · HTML

An ABANDONED authorized spin-off of GateKeepers ‘Futa Apocalypse’, this linear VN with GoR bad-ends centers on the experiences of a family in a dystopian future; plagued by a Futa Virus. Central themes are Futa/Trans… obviously… corruption, transformation, incest, serial-corruption, and betrayal of special trust. Each decision is win or lose, although that is not always apparent immediately. Each loss may not be your own. The whole family wins or loses, and you may experience the consequences from each point of view. Many paths are unfinished and contain a placeholder page. When you reach an end, back button to the previous choice, and play out all ends. Or restart and try a different path. Only one path… Good or evil… leads to the true abandoned temp end of the game @ day 3. Additional Information:
