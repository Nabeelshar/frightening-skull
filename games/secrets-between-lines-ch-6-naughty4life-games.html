Secrets Between Lines [Ch.6] [Naughty4Life Games]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-11-27 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

Secrets Between Lines is a story-driven visual novel set in the city of San Viero, a place where power, money, and secrets shape every aspect of life. After witnessing a mysterious murder, your life takes an unexpected turn. Desperate to support your sick mother, you accept a job working for one of the city’s most influential families. What begins as an opportunity to rebuild your life soon pulls you into a dangerous world of hidden agendas, organized crime, and conspiracies that reach far beyond anything you could have imagined. As you uncover the truth behind a series of strange events, you’ll meet a diverse cast of characters, each with their own ambitions, secrets, and motivations. Build relationships, earn trust, pursue romance, and decide who deserves your loyalty as the mystery surrounding San Viero slowly unfolds. Every choice matters. The people you support, the relationships you develop, and the secrets you choose to investigate will shape your journey and influence future events.
