8 Weeks [v0.3.1] [FromPlutoGuy]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-10-29 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

Overview: 8 Weeks is a visual novel about returning home after years away and trying to start over. Back in your hometown, you reconnect with people from your past and meet new ones, while your brother’s problems slowly begin to become your own. Every woman you meet has her own personality, past, and secrets waiting to be discovered. Each one has something to reveal. Your choices shape the relationships you build with the women around you, changing how they see you and how close they let you get.
