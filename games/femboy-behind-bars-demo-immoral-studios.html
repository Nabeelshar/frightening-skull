Femboy Behind Bars [demo] [Immoral Studios]

No rating yet · Thread Updated 2026-10-29 · Ongoing · Ren'Py

Cell 168, top bunk. The plan was simple: keep your head down, owe nobody, serve the time. Then a guard shoves a new cellmate through the door, ‘as agreed’, and the plan has about a week left. Your new cellmate is Sky, a blond with freckles who answers one word at a time and draws in a notebook he keeps closed. Brody runs the block and wants Sky moved to his tier. The guard can make the paperwork go away, once, for sixty cigarettes you don’t have.
