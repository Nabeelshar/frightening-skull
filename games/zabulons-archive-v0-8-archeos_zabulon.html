Zabulon’s Archive isn’t a visual novel. It’s many, MANY visual novels. Some are original stories, others are fictions involving numerous characters and universes. Some contain GIFs, others contain AI renders using a wide variety of artistic styles. All are smutty. Do you want to follow the story of a stripper reuniting with her grumpy high school teacher while she performs on stage? Or an Onlyfans model getting recognized by her boyfriend’s dad? A girl who has to pretend to be a love doll during a business meeting? Are you interested in seeing Gwen Stacy trying to wreck her mom’s marriage? Hermione “comforting” a heartbroken Hagrid? Momo Ayase having a one-night stand? Those stories and many more await you in the Archive. You just need to read them (harsh, I know).
