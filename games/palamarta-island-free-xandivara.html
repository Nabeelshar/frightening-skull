Palamarta Island is a story-driven slice-of-life game about starting over, discovering new connections, and finding a place to call home. After leaving the city behind, you arrive on a quiet island with only a handful of residents. Life here is very different from what you’re used to. The days are slower, the atmosphere is more relaxed, and the people you meet each have their own stories, personalities, and secrets. As you settle into island life, you’ll get to know its residents, build relationships, experience everyday moments, and uncover the stories hidden beneath the island’s peaceful surface.
