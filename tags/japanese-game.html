Fair-Skinned Big-Breasted Sister’s Curse Ritual Training [v1.2.0] [Mr. Delicatessen]

No rating yet · Updated 2026-11-08 · Ongoing

Elysia, a lone priestess, has been dispatched to a certain small town. Her organization, the “Eld Kingdom Church,” is a group that sends clergy to towns and villages sacrificed during the Great War to provide reconstruction support for those affected by the conflict. While Elysia was sent to this small town for that same purpose, she also carries a secret, simultaneous mission: “The tracking and discovery of fragments of cursed magical weapons used in the Great War.” Even a mere fragment of a cursed weapon possesses enough power to wipe out an entire town with a hex; unless the weapons themselves are removed, the number of victims will never decrease. However, if rumors were to spread that such items were located in “that town,” it would lead to secondary disasters—therefore, the Church has carried out these orders in strict secrecy. Thus, Elysia’s mission begins. But will she truly be able to return from this town unscathed…?
